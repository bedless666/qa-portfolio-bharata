📜 THE CHRONICLES 📜

The Tale of Liberation

In the land of the Scarlet Moon Empire, a young hero bearing the cursed Soul Eater Rune rises against tyranny. Gathering the legendary 108 Stars of Destiny, he builds an army of liberation from the ruins of Toran Castle. Each star brings unique talents—warriors, mages, strategists, and craftsmen—united by a common cause: freedom.

"The stars have aligned. Our destiny awaits at Toran Castle."

Years later, in the neighboring land of Dunan, another hero emerges. Betrayed by those he trusted, he must once again unite the 108 Stars to face the Highland Kingdom. The Bright Shield Rune guides his path as he builds an army capable of challenging an empire.

"This hand of mine glows with an awesome power!"

These are not mere tales of war, but stories of friendship, sacrifice, and the bonds that unite people across nations. The 108 Stars of Destiny are more than soldiers—they are artists, chefs, merchants, and dreamers, each playing their part in shaping history.
