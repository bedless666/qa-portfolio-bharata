## 💡 Pro Tips

### Notion Portfolio
1. **Start simple** - Launch MVP first, enhance later
2. **Use callout boxes** - Makes achievements stand out
3. **Add emojis** - Visual appeal without images
4. **Test on mobile** - Ensure mobile-friendly
5. **Get feedback** - Share with trusted colleague

### LinkedIn
1. **Update headline first** - Most visible element
2. **Add portfolio to Featured** - Prominent placement
3. **Use keywords** - AI-Augmented, Test Automation, Selenium
4. **Engage with content** - Like/comment on QA posts
5. **Post regularly** - 1-2 posts per week

### Job Search
1. **Quality over quantity** - Customize each application
2. **Network actively** - Many jobs filled through referrals
3. **Follow up** - Email hiring managers after applying
4. **Practice demos** - Be ready to walk through portfolio
5. **Track applications** - Spreadsheet with company, date, status
